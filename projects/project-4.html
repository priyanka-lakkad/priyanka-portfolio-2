01 · Project Overview

The Problem

Jaguar Racing needed a more compelling way to engage thousands of attendees during live Formula E race weekends. Event teams also lacked a centralized way to track audience engagement, content performance, and the operational status of the experience in real time.

The Solution

I designed a connected experience comprising two products: an interactive touchscreen platform for fans and a B2B operational tool for event teams. The fan experience transformed race-day content into an intuitive journey, while the internal platform enabled stakeholders to monitor engagement, manage dynamic content, and track performance across the event.

The Impact

The experience engaged more than 20,000 attendees at the London E-Prix and delivered a 31% faster setup time. It gave Jaguar Racing a scalable platform for connecting with fans while providing internal teams with greater visibility and control during live events.

My Role

As Interaction Designer, I led the end-to-end experience across both the customer-facing and B2B products. Working with Jaguar Racing stakeholders, creative partners, engineers, and event teams, I defined the product strategy, interaction models, user flows, information architecture, and touchscreen behaviors. I guided the work from concept through implementation, ensuring both experiences met the needs of fans, internal stakeholders, and live event operations.

04 · Reflection

Brief brand moment Meaningful fan connection

What Worked Well

This project reinforced how thoughtful interaction design can turn a brief brand moment into a meaningful connection. By making the car and the circuit tangible through touch, the experience gave thousands of fans at the London E-Prix a reason to stop, engage, and spend time with Jaguar Racing, while the operations tool gave the team the visibility to run it with confidence on race day.

Beyond race day
+ Pre-race anticipation Race-day touchscreen + Post-race follow-up + Season-long relationship

Next Steps

If I revisited the project, I would design for the relationship rather than the moment. I would explore how the experience could continue beyond race day, so that a single interaction at the track becomes the starting point for a longer-term relationship with the Jaguar brand across the season.
